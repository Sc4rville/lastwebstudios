# cloturiste — playbook (fences and gates / fencing)

> The **STRUCTURE and the concept come from HERE**. The trade decides the information architecture; fonts + archetype = flavour. Write the site in the agency's language (LANGUAGE).

## What this trade sells
**Privacy and security for the property**, and the look of the entrance (the gate). A visual purchase, a quick quote, often seasonal.

## Concept & angle
"Your land, well marked out, well finished". A practical, visual tone; the materials and the projects guide the choice.

## STRUCTURE — the trade's information architecture
1. **Hero**: an installed fence/gate, a promise, a free quote.
2. **Types**: wood, aluminium, wire mesh, composite, gates (motorisation).
3. **Projects**: a gallery by material.
4. **How it works**: measuring → quote → installation.
5. **Guarantees / reviews**.
6. **CTA**: quote.

## Mood
Outdoor, natural; green/wood or anthracite aluminium. A clean product grid.

## Avoid
A catalogue of panels with no real photo.

## Photos
The prospect's real jobs shown big; no stock images. With no usable photo: illustrate the trade (SVG, textures), invent nothing.
