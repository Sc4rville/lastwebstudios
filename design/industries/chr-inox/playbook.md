# chr-inox — playbook (professional stainless-steel equipment for hospitality) (READ by the composer AND the filler)

> The **STRUCTURE and the concept come from HERE**. The trade decides the information architecture; fonts + archetype = flavour.

## What this trade sells
**Bespoke stainless-steel equipment for professional kitchens**: sinks, worktops, hoods, units, labs.
A 100% B2B client (restaurants, institutions, bakeries, supermarkets), a technical and functional purchase. Ticket
€5-100k. The client buys **compliance, robustness, made to measure to the centimetre** and a supplier reliable over time.

## Concept & angle
**Bespoke stainless-steel manufacture** + compliance with **HACCP standards** + a workshop that produces fast and clean.
An industrial-professional tone, precise, reassuring on compliance and lead times. No lifestyle emotion: here we talk
hygiene, durability, integration to the centimetre. Sell the trade expertise ("we equip the professionals' kitchens").

## STRUCTURE — the trade's information architecture (≠ chr: here an equipment supplier, not the guest experience)
1. **Hero**: a spotless professional stainless-steel lab/kitchen + promise ("Bespoke stainless steel for professional
   kitchens") + CTA **"Request a quote / a plan"** + phone.
2. **What we make**: sinks, worktops, units, hoods, shelving, cold rooms (a clear catalogue).
3. **Bespoke & workshop**: design from a plan → stainless-steel manufacture → installation (the differentiator).
4. **Projects**: equipped professional kitchens (restaurants, institutions) — the proof that reassures pros, shown big.
5. **Compliance & quality**: HACCP standards, 304/316 stainless steel, clean welding, after-sales (the professional
   buyer's language).
6. **Client references**: brands/establishments served, logos — a pledge of B2B seriousness.
7. **Final CTA**: "Request a quote" / "Study my professional kitchen project".

## Mood
Industrial-professional, crisp and cold. A technical grotesque (Suisse Intl, GT America, Fakt, Monument Grotesk) × mono
for specs/standards. A steel/stainless palette, cold grey, 1 accent. Minimal motion, crisp reveals — rigour > effect.

## Avoid
The "warm restaurant" tone (that is the CLIENT, not them — see chr). A gallery of dishes. Lifestyle emotion.
Vague promises: a professional buyer wants dimensions, standards, lead times, references.

## Photos
Real stainless-steel equipment in the workshop and installed (professional kitchens, labs), welding/finish details,
crisp views. The real workshop/job > 3D renders or stock stainless steel.
