# menuiserie — playbook (READ by the composer AND the filler)

> The **STRUCTURE and the concept come from HERE**. The trade decides the information architecture; fonts + archetype = flavour.

## What this trade sells
**Joinery**: windows, doors, shutters, gates, walk-in wardrobes, staircases, wood floors — wood, aluminium, PVC. Two
worlds: **exterior** joinery (windows/doors, an energy-performance purchase + grants) and bespoke **interior wood
fit-out** (wardrobe/staircase). Ticket €2-25k. The choice rests on quality, insulation, made to measure, grants.

## Concept & angle
**Made to measure that fits perfectly** + **performance** (insulation, comfort, security). A qualified craftsman's
tone, precise and warm. Sell measuring to the millimetre, beautiful wood/aluminium installed cleanly, and — for the
exterior — energy savings + grants (MaPrimeRénov' in France). Set it apart from the big-box store through real
bespoke work and installation.

## STRUCTURE — the trade's information architecture (≠ agencement: here the piece of joinery, not the whole space)
1. **Hero**: a beautiful installation (windows, wardrobe, staircase) + promise ("Bespoke joinery in <area>") +
   CTA **"Free quote"** + phone.
2. **Services**: exterior joinery (windows/doors/shutters/gates), interior fit-out (wardrobe/staircase/floors).
3. **Materials**: wood, aluminium, PVC, mixed — the advantages of each (orients and reassures on the choice).
4. **Projects**: a gallery of real installations (window before/after, wardrobes) — the proof, shown big.
5. **Performance & grants**: insulation, comfort, security, MaPrimeRénov'/CEE in France (a strong trigger for the
   exterior).
6. **Trust**: RGE, 10-year liability insurance, manufacture/workshop, free quote, testimonials.
7. **Final CTA**: "Free quote" / "Study my project".

## Mood
A qualified craftsman, crisp and warm. A clean grotesque (Geist, Onest, Telegraf, Suisse Intl) × a sans, or a discreet
serif for fit-out. A light wood/anthracite/white palette, 1 accent. Sober motion, crisp reveals, material.

## Avoid
An "impersonal big-box store" tone. Mixing exterior and interior with no hierarchy (make the two worlds clear).
Hiding RGE/grants (the exterior trigger). Confusing it with metallerie (structural aluminium) or agencement (the whole
space).

## Photos
Real installations: windows/doors/shutters fitted, bespoke wardrobes and staircases, floors. Strong before/after.
Local projects > catalogue renders or stock photo.
