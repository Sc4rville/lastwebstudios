# construction — playbook (READ by the composer AND the filler)

> The **STRUCTURE and the concept come from HERE**. The trade decides the information architecture; fonts + archetype = flavour.

## What this trade sells
**Building construction** (houses, structural work, heavy renovation, masonry extensions). A very big ticket
(€100-500k), a huge commitment from the client → **trust** comes before everything. The client chooses on the
projects, the guarantees and the company's solidity (fear of a job that goes off the rails).

## Concept & angle
**Control of the job from A to Z** + **the company's solidity** ("we build, we keep to deadlines, we guarantee").
A serious, solid, reassuring builder's tone. Sell peace of mind on a life project: a single point of contact,
deadlines, compliance, 10-year liability insurance. The proof = delivered buildings.

## STRUCTURE — the trade's information architecture (≠ construction-bois: here general masonry/structural work)
1. **Hero**: a strong delivered building/house + promise ("Builder in <area>") + CTA **"Start my project /
   Quote"** + phone.
2. **What we build**: new house, extension, heavy renovation, structural work (orients the visitor).
3. **Projects**: a gallery of delivered jobs (before/after very strong for renovation) — the main proof.
4. **A controlled job**: study → permit → foundations/structural work → finishing trades → handover.
5. **Trust & guarantees**: 10-year liability insurance (the French décennale), insurance, qualifications
   (Qualibat/RGE in France), a single point of contact.
6. **Testimonials**: reassured clients (a big job = fear, a review defuses it).
7. **Final CTA**: "Study my project" / quote / appointment.

## Mood
A solid, reliable builder. A sturdy grotesque (GT America, Monument Grotesk, Suisse Intl, Fakt) × a clean sans,
a crisp grid. A concrete/anthracite palette + 1 frank accent. Sober motion, structured reveals, seriousness > spectacle.

## Avoid
A "lifestyle/decor" tone. Vague promises with no projects. Confusing it with civil works (roads/networks) or
construction-bois (the timber sector). Hiding guarantees/qualifications (that is what reassures on a big ticket).

## Photos
Delivered buildings/houses shown big, jobs in progress (crane, structural work), renovation before/after.
Real local projects > renders or stock images.
