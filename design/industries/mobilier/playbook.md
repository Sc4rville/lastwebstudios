# mobilier — playbook (READ by the composer AND the filler)

> The **STRUCTURE and the concept come from HERE**. The trade decides the information architecture; fonts + archetype = flavour.

## What this trade sells
**Furniture**: designing/making furniture (bespoke cabinetmaking, or a design edition/collection, or professional
furniture for businesses). An **aesthetic, desire-driven** purchase: you fall in love with a piece. Ticket
€0.5-15k per piece. The client chooses on **style**, material and signature. A very visual sale, almost a catalogue
of objects.

## Concept & angle
**The beautiful object that lasts**: a piece designed, handmade or carefully edited, with a real identity. A design
studio / cabinetmaker tone, desirable and material. Sell the object as a piece you want to own + the craft/noble
materials. Put the piece on a pedestal (close-ups, light, material).

## STRUCTURE — the trade's information architecture (≠ agencement: here the object/piece, not the space nor the installation)
1. **Product hero**: THE signature piece shown big, beautifully lit + promise/identity + CTA **"See the
   collection / Made to measure"**.
2. **Collection / catalogue**: the pieces by family (chairs, tables, storage…) — the gallery of objects reigns.
3. **Made to measure**: a piece thought through with the client (woods, finishes) — when applicable.
4. **Craft & materials**: workshop, wood/metal/leather, finishes (the perceived value, in detail).
5. **Identity / story**: the studio, the inspiration, the manifesto (desire comes from the story).
6. **Trust**: noble materials, manufacture, lead times, delivery, testimonials/press.
7. **Final CTA**: "Discover the collection" / "Order / made to measure".

## Mood
A desirable design studio, material. An editorial serif or a chic grotesque (Reckless, Canela, Signifier, Whyte) × a
clean sans, big white space. A neutral material palette + 1 strong accent. Slow motion, elegant reveals, close-ups of
material.

## Avoid
A "discount furniture store" tone. Small/badly lit photos (the object MUST shine). Too much text instead of letting
the object speak. Confusing it with agencement (space) or menuiserie (built-in installation).

## Photos
The furniture pieces shown big, perfectly lit (studio + in situ), material/finish details.
The real pieces > generic 3D renders.
