# lieu-reception — playbook (READ by the composer AND the filler)

> The **STRUCTURE and the concept come from HERE** (not from a generic skeleton). The trade decides the information
> architecture; the font pair + the visual archetype are only a flavour on top.

## What this trade sells
A **setting for the most beautiful day**. The couple chooses ~90% on the **photos** and the **vibe**. A huge ticket
(€15-30k per event) → the visitor wants to **picture themselves there**, not compare specs. The venue's name = the brand.

## Concept & angle
Sell **an emotion + a place**. A **warm, elegant, evocative** tone (never corporate/"showroom").
Evoke the moment ("saying yes under the plane trees", "the party until the end of the night"), the light, the scale.

## STRUCTURE — the trade's information architecture (the order matters, it is WHAT sets it apart from the kitchen maker)
1. **Full-screen immersive hero**: the estate's most beautiful photo + the promise of a place + name + region
   ("Wedding estate in Provence · 30 min from Lyon") + a strong CTA **"Check a date / Book a visit"**.
2. **The place**: the building, the grounds, the spaces (hall, chapel, suites) — short text, wide photos.
3. **Capacity & spaces**: guests seated/standing, indoor/outdoor, rain plan B (clear figures).
4. **The GALLERY reigns**: big, immersive, real weddings — **it is the heart**, give it the most space.
5. **Packages / services**: exclusivity, on-site accommodation, caterer of choice or imposed.
6. **Couples' testimonials**: social proof = huge reassurance on this ticket.
7. **Final CTA**: "Check a date" / availability calendar.

## Mood (a flavour, the composer chooses from the catalogue)
Editorial-cinematic, warm. An elegant serif for display (Canela, Stempel Garamond, PP Fragment Serif)
× a clean sans. A soft/golden/nature palette. Slow motion, wide fades, parallax on the venue photos.

## Avoid
Product/craftsman vocabulary ("made to measure", "installation", "materials quote"). Walls of text (one evocative
line > a paragraph). Lukewarm CTAs ("contact us").

## Photos
The most **atmospheric** ones (light, grounds, the hall set up, the overall view) in the hero + full width.
Real weddings > empty photos.
