# charpente — playbook (READ by the composer AND the filler)

> The **STRUCTURE and the concept come from HERE**. The trade decides the information architecture; fonts + archetype = flavour.

## What this trade sells
The **timber structure of a building**: traditional roof framing, trusses, timber frame, raising, extension.
Ticket €10-80k, often upstream of a house/renovation project. The client (a homeowner or a builder) buys
**structural expertise** and trust in a piece that holds the roof up for 100 years.

## Concept & angle
The **craft of load-bearing timber**: precise cutting, tradition + CNC machinery, solidity. A tone of craft
excellence, serious and proud of the trade. Sell the technical mastery (calculation, lifting) and the beauty of an
exposed frame. A master craftsman title (Compagnon / Meilleur Ouvrier de France) is hugely reassuring.

## STRUCTURE — the trade's information architecture (≠ roofing: here the frame, not the roof's weatherproofing)
1. **Hero**: a magnificent frame (exposed, being lifted, the cutting workshop) + promise ("Timber framer in
   <area>") + CTA **"Request a quote"** + phone.
2. **The craft**: traditional vs industrial framing, workshop cutting, lifting (the technical heart).
3. **Services**: new build, renovation/restoration, raising, timber-frame extension, exposed framing.
4. **Projects**: jobs (before/during/after lifting) — a raised frame impresses, shown big.
5. **The workshop & the team**: cutting machines, selected timber, a qualified team (a pledge of precision).
6. **Trust**: 10-year liability insurance (the French décennale), trade qualification (Qualibat), certified timber,
   testimonials.
7. **Final CTA**: "Study my roof frame" / quote.

## Mood
Noble craft, warm-raw. A solid serif or a sturdy grotesque (Reckless, Signifier, TWK Everett, Telegraf)
× mono for technical labels. A wood/oak palette, warm brown, anthracite. Sober motion, crisp reveals, material.

## Avoid
A "decor/lifestyle" tone (this is structure). Confusing it with roofing (the roof) or timber construction
(the whole house). Hiding the qualifications. Stock photos of wood instead of the real jobs.

## Photos
Real frames: lifting, exposed framing, the cutting workshop, joints. Worked timber shown big.
Before/during/after of a job > stock photo.
