# agencement — playbook (READ by the composer AND the filler)

> The **STRUCTURE and the concept come from HERE**. The trade decides the information architecture; fonts + archetype = flavour.

## What this trade sells
**Bespoke space fit-out** for businesses (shops, restaurants, offices, hotels) and high-end homeowners: design +
manufacture + installation of a complete space. Ticket €20-200k, a long decision, often B2B.
The client buys **end-to-end control** (from the idea to a turnkey handover) and a style.

## Concept & angle
The **space thought through from end to end** ("we design, we build, we install"). A studio/interior-architect tone,
premium and controlled. Sell the coherence of a signature space, not a single piece of furniture. Put the workshop and
the design office forward — it is the differentiator against a mere installer.

## STRUCTURE — the trade's information architecture (≠ joinery: here a whole space project, not one piece)
1. **Editorial hero**: one strong fitted space (a signature shop/restaurant) + promise ("Bespoke fit-out for
   <sector>") + CTA **"Talk about your project"**.
2. **Sectors served**: retail, hospitality, offices, residential (the visitor recognises themselves).
3. **The turnkey method**: design/3D → design office → workshop manufacture → installation → handover.
4. **Projects**: a gallery of delivered spaces by type — the main proof, shown big.
5. **The workshop & the craft**: machines, materials, team (reassures about "made in-house").
6. **Trust**: brand references, deadlines met, business client testimonials.
7. **Final CTA**: "Study my project" / a qualified contact request.

## Mood
Premium studio, architectural. A crisp grotesque (Neue Haas, GT America, Suisse Intl, Geist) × a clean sans, big
white space, a strict grid. A neutral/concrete palette + 1 sober accent. Discreet motion, precise reveals, no gadgets.

## Avoid
A cobbled-together "local craftsman" tone. Tiny galleries (the spaces MUST breathe). Confusing it with plain joinery
(here it is the whole space). Technical jargon before the emotion of the place.

## Photos
Fitted spaces shown big (delivered shops, restaurants, offices), overall views + material/finish details.
Real local work > generic 3D renders.
