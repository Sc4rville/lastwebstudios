# desinsectisation — playbook (rodent and insect control / pest control)

> The **STRUCTURE and the concept come from HERE**. The trade decides the information architecture; fonts + archetype = flavour. Write the site in the agency's language (LANGUAGE).

## What this trade sells
**Peace of mind restored** against pests (rats, bedbugs, wasps, termites). Urgency and discretion; recurring contracts for businesses.

## Concept & angle
"Fast, discreet, guaranteed call-outs". A calm, expert tone, never alarming or disgusting.

## STRUCTURE — the trade's information architecture
1. **Hero**: promise + response time + call / quote.
2. **Pests treated**: rodents, bedbugs, insects, wasps, termites (cards).
3. **Method**: diagnosis → treatment → follow-up → guarantee.
4. **Businesses & contracts** (the recurring part).
5. **Trust**: approval, products, reviews.
6. **CTA**.

## Mood
Sober, clean, reassuring; green/blue, no repulsive image.

## Avoid
Close-ups of insects. An alarmist tone.

## Photos
The prospect's real jobs shown big; no stock images. With no usable photo: illustrate the trade (SVG, textures), invent nothing.
