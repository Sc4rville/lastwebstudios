# entrepreneur-general — playbook (general contractor)

> The **STRUCTURE and the concept come from HERE**. The trade decides the information architecture; fonts + archetype = flavour. Write the site in the agency's language (LANGUAGE).

## What this trade sells
The **complete project** (renovation, extension, construction) with a single point of contact. A big ticket, a long decision; the portfolio and trust do everything.

## Concept & angle
"One point of contact from the plans to the handover of the keys". A solid, precise tone; show the method and the projects.

## STRUCTURE — the trade's information architecture
1. **Hero**: a finished job + promise + project study.
2. **Projects**: the star section, by type (renovation, extension, new build) with before/after.
3. **Our method**: study → detailed quote → schedule → follow-up → handover.
4. **Trades** covered (masonry, plumbing, electrical…) without a catalogue.
5. **Trust**: guarantees, insurance, years, reviews.
6. **CTA**: talk about my project.

## Mood
Architectural, sober, wide photos; an assertive display font, a mineral palette (grey, sand, black).

## Avoid
The catch-all "every trade" site with no images. Prices per square metre.

## Photos
The prospect's real jobs shown big; no stock images. With no usable photo: illustrate the trade (SVG, textures), invent nothing.
