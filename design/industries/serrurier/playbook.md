# serrurier — playbook (locksmith)

> The **STRUCTURE and the concept come from HERE**. The trade decides the information architecture; fonts + archetype = flavour. Write the site in the agency's language (LANGUAGE).

## What this trade sells
**Emergencies** (door slammed shut, lost key) and **security** (reinforced doors, locks). A decision in seconds from a phone: the call is everything.

## Concept & angle
"Open now, price given, no damage". A crisp, honest tone (a sector riddled with scams: transparency sells).

## STRUCTURE — the trade's information architecture
1. **Call hero**: availability, arrival time, price given, a giant call button.
2. **Call-outs**: door opening, lock change, reinforcement, key copies.
3. **Clear prices**: a "from" price list if provided.
4. **Trust**: a local company, reviews, insurance, the craftsman's name.
5. **Area**.
6. **Call CTA**.

## Mood
Very readable, high-contrast, mobile-first; a warm accent or a sober red.

## Avoid
An anonymous site with no address (it looks like the scams).

## Photos
The prospect's real jobs shown big; no stock images. With no usable photo: illustrate the trade (SVG, textures), invent nothing.
