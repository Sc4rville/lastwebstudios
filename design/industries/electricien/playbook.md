# electricien — playbook (electrician)

> The **STRUCTURE and the concept come from HERE**. The trade decides the information architecture; fonts + archetype = flavour. Write the site in the agency's language (LANGUAGE).

## What this trade sells
**Safety and compliance** (bringing up to code, the panel, repairs) and projects (home automation, EV charger, renovation). A variable ticket, trust is decisive.

## Concept & angle
"A qualified electrician, work up to code, a clear price". Serious, crisp, reassuring; put qualifications and insurance forward.

## STRUCTURE — the trade's information architecture
1. **Hero**: promise + qualification (e.g. "certified electrician") + area + call / quote.
2. **Services**: repairs, bringing up to code, panel, home automation, EV charger, lighting.
3. **Why us**: standards, insurance, guarantee, lead times.
4. **Projects**: before/after photos of panels, installations, lighting.
5. **Area** + **reviews**.
6. **CTA**: free quote / call.

## Mood
Technical but warm; yellow/amber as the accent or deep blue. A crisp grid, simple icons.

## Avoid
Blocks of regulatory jargon. Photos of anonymous cables.

## Photos
The prospect's real jobs shown big; no stock images. With no usable photo: illustrate the trade (SVG, textures), invent nothing.
