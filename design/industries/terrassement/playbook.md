# terrassement — playbook (READ by the composer AND the filler)

> The **STRUCTURE and the concept come from HERE**. The trade decides the information architecture; fonts + archetype = flavour.

## What this trade sells
**Earthworks**: site preparation, excavation, foundations, utility connections, drainage, demolition, platforms,
rock work. A homeowner client (before a build/pool) or a business/construction client. Ticket €3-50k. A **rational,
trust-based** purchase: you hand your plot to heavy machinery. The choice rests on the equipment, seriousness, the
quote.

## Concept & angle
**The equipped, reliable earthworks contractor**: the right machine, clean work, by the rules, on time. A solid,
down-to-earth construction tone, reassuring about control (machinery, removal, compliance). Sell capacity (the
machine fleet) + seriousness (soil study, respect of the rules, a clean job site). No aesthetics: concrete facts.

## STRUCTURE — the trade's information architecture (≠ paysagiste: here the heavy ground work, not the finished garden)
1. **Hero**: a machine at work / ground in progress + promise ("Earthworks in <area>") + CTA **"Free quote"** + phone.
2. **Services**: earthworks, excavation/foundations, utility connections, drainage, demolition, rock work.
3. **The machine fleet**: excavators, mini-excavators, trucks (capacity = a strong concrete argument).
4. **Projects / jobs**: platforms, prepared ground (ground before/after) — the proof, shown big.
5. **A controlled job**: study → access → earthworks → removal → left clean (reassures the homeowner).
6. **Trust**: 10-year liability insurance, qualifications, respect of rules/soils, area, testimonials.
7. **Final CTA**: "Free quote" / "Study my land".

## Mood
Solid construction, controlled rawness. A sturdy grotesque (GT America, Monument Grotesk, Fakt, Suisse Intl) × mono
for figures. An earth/ochre/anthracite palette + 1 job-site accent (yellow/orange). Sober motion, crisp reveals, mass.

## Avoid
A "lifestyle/garden" tone (this is heavy work — see paysagiste). Empty pages with no machines or jobs.
Confusing it with tp (roads/networks on a larger scale) or construction. A vague quote (the client fears extra costs).

## Photos
Machines at work (excavators, trucks), ground and platforms (before/after), excavation, rock work. Shown big.
The real jobs > generic machinery stock images.
