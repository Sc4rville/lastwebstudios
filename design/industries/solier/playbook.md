# solier — playbook (floors and wood flooring / flooring)

> The **STRUCTURE and the concept come from HERE**. The trade decides the information architecture; fonts + archetype = flavour. Write the site in the agency's language (LANGUAGE).

## What this trade sells
**The material underfoot**: wood flooring, tiling, floor renovation. An aesthetic and comfort purchase, decided through images and finishes.

## Concept & angle
"A floor that changes the whole room". A warm, material tone; close-ups of textures and laying patterns.

## STRUCTURE — the trade's information architecture
1. **Hero**: a laid floor in the light + promise + quote.
2. **Materials**: solid wood, laminate, tiles, stone, carpet.
3. **Projects**: before/after and laying details.
4. **Renovation**: sanding, varnishing (possibly recurring).
5. **Reviews / guarantees**.
6. **CTA**.

## Mood
Warm, textures, wood and stone; editorial typography.

## Avoid
Lists of supplier references.

## Photos
The prospect's real jobs shown big; no stock images. With no usable photo: illustrate the trade (SVG, textures), invent nothing.
