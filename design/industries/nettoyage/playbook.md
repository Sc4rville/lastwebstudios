# nettoyage — playbook (cleaning company)

> The **STRUCTURE and the concept come from HERE**. The trade decides the information architecture; fonts + archetype = flavour. Write the site in the agency's language (LANGUAGE).

## What this trade sells
A **regular, reliable service** (offices, apartment buildings, homes, windows). Recurring contracts: the site reassures and makes the quote easy.

## Concept & angle
"Clean, regular, without you having to think about it". A professional, fresh tone; reliability and flexibility count more than image.

## STRUCTURE — the trade's information architecture
1. **Hero**: promise + types of clients + quote within 24 h.
2. **Services**: offices, apartment buildings, post-construction, windows, homes.
3. **Why us**: trained team, insurance, products, flexible hours.
4. **Clients / reviews**.
5. **Area**.
6. **Quote CTA**.

## Mood
Fresh, light, lots of air; light blue/mint green or neutral.

## Avoid
Stock photos of yellow gloves. ISO jargon.

## Photos
The prospect's real jobs shown big; no stock images. With no usable photo: illustrate the trade (SVG, textures), invent nothing.
