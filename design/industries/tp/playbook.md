# tp — playbook (public works / civil engineering) (READ by the composer AND the filler)

> The **STRUCTURE and the concept come from HERE**. The trade decides the information architecture; fonts + archetype = flavour.

## What this trade sells
**Public works**: roads, networks (VRD), drainage, civil engineering, urban development, structures.
A mostly **public/business** client (local authorities, developers, industry), often through **public
contracts/tenders**. Big tickets. A **rational, demanding** purchase: technical capacity, references, certifications,
safety, respect of deadlines and of the environment. The site serves institutional credibility, not the emotional
"lead".

## Concept & angle
**The capable, responsible public-works company**: resources, references, safety, environment, deadlines kept. A
corporate-industrial, solid, institutional tone. Sell the capacity to carry big jobs + reliability (QSE,
certifications, public references). Credibility and seriousness come before aesthetics or emotion.

## STRUCTURE — the trade's information architecture (≠ terrassement: here public-works/tender scale, infrastructure)
1. **Hero**: a large-scale structure/job (road, network, structure) + promise ("Public works & utilities") +
   CTA **"Consult us / Contact"** (B2B/tenders, not a general-public "free quote").
2. **Areas of expertise**: roads, utilities/networks, drainage, civil engineering, urban development.
3. **Projects / references**: delivered jobs (public and private) — the proof of capacity, shown big.
4. **Resources & capacity**: equipment fleet, teams, key figures (km laid, m³…) — large-scale credibility.
5. **QSE & commitments**: safety, quality, environment, certifications (ISO, MASE…) — decisive in tenders.
6. **The company**: years in business, locations, HR/recruitment, institutional clients (reassurance).
7. **Final CTA**: "Consult us" / contact + a tenders/recruitment area.

## Mood
Corporate-industrial, institutional and solid. A serious grotesque (Suisse Intl, GT America, Fakt, Inter) × mono for
figures. An industrial palette (blue/grey/anthracite + a safety accent). Sober motion, crisp reveals, data.

## Avoid
A "local craftsman / general-public free quote" tone (the target is business/public). Lifestyle emotion. Confusing it
with terrassement (a smaller scale). Pages with no references or QSE (that is what builds credibility in a tender).

## Photos
Real large-scale jobs and structures (roads, networks, civil engineering), machinery, teams, wide views. Shown big.
Real projects + figures > generic institutional stock images.
