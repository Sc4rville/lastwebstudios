# peintre — playbook (house painter / painter)

> The **STRUCTURE and the concept come from HERE**. The trade decides the information architecture; fonts + archetype = flavour. Write the site in the agency's language (LANGUAGE).

## What this trade sells
**A visible transformation**: the before/after of a room or a facade. An aesthetic purchase, decided through images and trust (a clean job site).

## Concept & angle
Sell the result: "a house that breathes". A polished, calm tone; the site must be as clean as the job promised.

## STRUCTURE — the trade's information architecture
1. **Image hero**: a finished, bright room + promise + quote.
2. **Before / after**: the star section (sliders or pairs).
3. **Services**: interior, exterior/facade renovation, decoration, wallpaper, plastering.
4. **How we work**: protection, cleanliness, deadlines kept.
5. **Reviews** + **gallery**.
6. **CTA**: free quote with a visit.

## Mood
Bright, lots of white, a soft accent colour (terracotta, sage). Elegant type, big photos.

## Avoid
Lists of paints by brand. A dark background.

## Photos
The prospect's real jobs shown big; no stock images. With no usable photo: illustrate the trade (SVG, textures), invent nothing.
