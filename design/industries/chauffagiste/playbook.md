# chauffagiste — playbook (heating engineer / HVAC)

> The **STRUCTURE and the concept come from HERE**. The trade decides the information architecture; fonts + archetype = flavour. Write the site in the agency's language (LANGUAGE).

## What this trade sells
**Comfort all year round** and **the energy bill**: heat pump, air conditioning, boiler, servicing. A medium to large ticket, grants/financing, and a recurring service contract.

## Concept & angle
"Warm in winter, cool in summer, cheaper every month". An expert, reassuring tone; recurring servicing is the business asset to highlight.

## STRUCTURE — the trade's information architecture
1. **Hero**: comfort + savings promise, area, call / free assessment.
2. **Solutions**: heat pump, air conditioning, boiler, underfloor heating (with grants if relevant).
3. **Servicing & repairs**: contracts, fast call-outs (the recurring part).
4. **Projects** + figures (installations, years).
5. **Proof**: certifications (RGE in France…), brands installed, reviews.
6. **CTA**: assessment / quote.

## Mood
Clean, technical-premium: cold blue + a warm accent. Clear pictograms, figures put forward.

## Avoid
A catalogue of technical data sheets. Forgetting servicing.

## Photos
The prospect's real jobs shown big; no stock images. With no usable photo: illustrate the trade (SVG, textures), invent nothing.
