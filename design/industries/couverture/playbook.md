# couverture — playbook (roofer / roofing) (READ by the composer AND the filler)

> The **STRUCTURE and the concept come from HERE**. The trade decides the information architecture; fonts + archetype = flavour.

## What this trade sells
**The roof**: installation, renovation, repair, waterproofing, zinc work, moss removal. Ticket €3-30k; often an
**emergency** (leak, storm) or an energy renovation. The client chooses on **responsiveness**, seriousness and trust
(fear of the door-to-door "roof cleaning" scam). A lot of **local + emergency**.

## Concept & angle
**The serious, responsive roofer**: a watertight, clean roof that lasts — with no nasty surprise. A reliable,
down-to-earth, reassuring craftsman's tone. Sell peace of mind ("your roof in good hands") + fast call-outs. Fight
the trade's mistrust: a clear quote, guarantees, an identified local company.

## STRUCTURE — the trade's information architecture (≠ charpente: here the roof's envelope/waterproofing, not the frame)
1. **Hero**: a beautiful redone roof + promise ("Roofer in <area>") + CTA **"Free quote / Leak emergency"** + a
   **clearly visible phone** (emergencies convert by phone).
2. **Services**: roof installation/renovation, waterproofing, zinc work, moss removal/cleaning, leak repair.
3. **Emergency & responsiveness**: fast call-outs for leaks/storms (trigger #1 — put it forward).
4. **Projects**: roofs before/after (moss removal and renovation = very visual) — the proof.
5. **Trust**: 10-year liability insurance, insurance, a local company, a free and clear quote (anti-scam).
6. **Testimonials / service area**: reviews + towns covered (local reassures and helps search).
7. **Final CTA**: "Free quote" / "Call us".

## Mood
A reliable, straightforward craftsman. A solid, readable grotesque (Geist, Telegraf, Onest, Fakt) × a clean sans, crisp
contrast. A slate/tile, blue-grey palette, 1 trust accent. Sober motion, crisp reveals, readability above all.

## Avoid
A "luxury/editorial" tone (it is a reassurance purchase, not a dream). Hiding the phone (emergencies call).
Confusing it with charpente. Empty galleries: the roof before/after is the strongest argument.

## Photos
Real roofs before/after, jobs (scaffolding, laying tiles/slates/zinc), moss removal. Shown big.
Real local projects > a generic roof stock photo.
