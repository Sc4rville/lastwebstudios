# chr — playbook (café-hotel-restaurant) (READ by the composer AND the filler)

> The **STRUCTURE and the concept come from HERE**. The trade decides the information architecture; fonts + archetype = flavour.

## What this trade sells
An **experience to live on site**: a café, a hotel or a restaurant. The client chooses on the **atmosphere**, the
**menu / the rooms** and the **reviews**. A low ticket per visit but strong repeat business; the site's goal = get
people to **come and book**. The place = the brand; the photos and the vibe do everything.

## Concept & angle
Sell a **craving** ("we want to be there"). A warm, sensory, generous tone — never corporate. Evoke the taste, the
light, the welcome. The goal: the visitor **books a table / a room** or finds the address.

## STRUCTURE — the trade's information architecture (≠ chr-inox: here we sell the experience, not professional equipment)
1. **Immersive hero**: the most beautiful photo of the place/a dish + name + the promise of a moment + a visible
   **booking button** (and phone/address).
2. **The atmosphere / the place**: the dining room, the terrace, the spirit — short text, warm full-width photos.
3. **The menu / the rooms**: a signature menu or room types (make them hungry/eager, not a spreadsheet).
4. **A sensory gallery**: dishes, plating, decor, moments — makes the mouth water.
5. **Practical info**: opening hours, address + map, booking, contact (the local conversion).
6. **Reviews & proof**: rating, testimonials, press/guides — reassurance.
7. **Final CTA**: "Book" / "See the menu" / directions.

## Mood
Warm, appetising, lively. An editorial serif (Canela, Reckless, Times Now, Stempel Garamond) × a clean sans, or a
friendly grotesque. A warm/earthy palette depending on the place's identity. Gentle motion, fades, photo parallax.

## Avoid
An "equipment / professional supplier" tone (that is chr-inox). Menus unreadable as images. Lukewarm CTAs
("contact us" instead of "book"). Walls of text instead of photos that make people want to come.

## Photos
The most appetising/atmospheric ones: dishes, dining room, terrace, rooms, light. In the hero + full width.
The real place > generic food stock images.
