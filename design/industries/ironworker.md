# Industry profile — ironworker (architectural metalwork)

> One file = one industry. Read by the `copywriter` skill before it writes a line. Encodes what a
> visitor of this trade needs to feel, in which order — the human "taste" the atelier accumulates.

## Who buys
Homeowners renovating a townhouse or loft, architects, interior designers. They buy **craft they can
see**: finished pieces, materials, the workshop. Price comes after trust.

## What the page must prove, in order
1. **Material honesty** — steel, brass, stainless: name the grade, show the finish.
2. **The hand** — the forge, the people, the size of the workshop.
3. **Precision** — survey, drawings, tolerances. Metalwork is millimetric.
4. **The path to a quote** — one clear action, repeated at the top and at the end.

## Tone
Short, confident, concrete. Nouns over adjectives. No "passion", no "savoir-faire d'exception".

## Visual cues
Dark, warm, industrial. One incandescent accent (taken from the brand). Condensed display type, a
technical mono for specs. Photography carries the emotion; typography carries the precision.
