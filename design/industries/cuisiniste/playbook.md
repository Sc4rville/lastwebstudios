# cuisiniste — playbook (READ by the composer AND the filler)

> The **STRUCTURE and the concept come from HERE**. The trade decides the information architecture; fonts + archetype = flavour.

## What this trade sells
**High-end bespoke work** + a **craft** + a **showroom**. Ticket €10-30k. The client researches online for a long time,
compares style and trust, then comes to the showroom. A sale made through imagery.

## Concept & angle
**Made to measure** ("your kitchen, not flat-pack furniture") + **end-to-end control** (3D design → manufacture →
installation). A premium, precise, reassuring tone. Put the workshop/showroom and the brands forward.

## STRUCTURE — the trade's information architecture (≠ lieu-reception: here process + product proof)
1. **Hero**: one strong project (a beautiful kitchen) + promise ("Bespoke kitchens in <town>") + CTA
   **"Book an appointment / Get a kitchen design"** + phone.
2. **Made to measure / the process**: 3D design → manufacture → installation, in 3-4 clear steps (the differentiator).
3. **Projects**: a gallery of installed kitchens (before/after if available) — the proof that converts.
4. **Partner brands** (appliances, worktops): a clean band = a pledge of quality.
5. **Workshop / showroom**: an invitation to come and see/touch (+ area, opening hours).
6. **Guarantees / financing**: reassurance (10-year guarantee, free quote, payment).
7. **Final CTA**: showroom appointment / simulate.

## Mood
Premium product, crisp. A grotesque with character (Neue Haas, Monument, Mier A, Telegraf) × a clean sans + mono for
specs/labels. Palette: neutrals + 1 brand accent. Sober motion, crisp reveals.

## Avoid
"Venue/event" vocabulary. Tiny galleries (the kitchen MUST be shown big). Inventing brands/labels that are not in the
fiche.

## Photos
Completed kitchens shown big (hero + gallery), material details. Real > generic 3D renders.
