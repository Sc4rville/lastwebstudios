# piscine — playbook (READ by the composer AND the filler)

> The **STRUCTURE and the concept come from HERE**. The trade decides the information architecture; fonts + archetype = flavour.

## What this trade sells
**A way of life**: the dream garden, summers with the family. A big ticket (€10-100k), a purchase heavily
researched online, an emotional decision. **Maintenance/after-sales = recurring revenue** (highlight it).

## Concept & angle
Sell **the dream + peace of mind** ("dive into your summer", "a pool, zero worries"). An aspirational but reassuring
tone (it is a big job at their home). The finished pool = the star.

## STRUCTURE — the trade's information architecture (≠ cuisiniste: here aspiration + a controlled job + recurring)
1. **Aspirational hero**: a beautiful pool (water, light, lifestyle) + promise + area + CTA **"My project / Free quote"**.
2. **Types of pools**: in-ground/concrete, shell, natural, lap pool… (orients the visitor).
3. **Projects**: a gallery of pools (before/after ground → pool = very strong here).
4. **A controlled project**: study → excavation → construction → filling (reassures about the big job).
5. **Maintenance & after-sales**: contracts, winterising, renovation (the recurring part — a business differentiator).
6. **Trust**: guarantees, labels (Propiscines in France…), testimonials.
7. **Final CTA**: "Study my project" / quote.

## Mood
Aspirational, fresh, natural. A clean/elegant display (Garnett, Geist, Onest, Telegraf, Whyte); a water-blue / greens /
sand palette depending on the pool. Fluid motion, light parallax on the water, soft reveals.

## Avoid
"Venue/showroom" vocabulary. The "catalogue of parts" feel. Hiding the after-sales/maintenance (it is an asset).

## Photos
Finished pools shown big (water, light, lifestyle), ground before/after. Real local work > stock images.
