# outillage — playbook (READ by the composer AND the filler)

> The **STRUCTURE and the concept come from HERE**. The trade decides the information architecture; fonts + archetype = flavour.

## What this trade sells
**Tools**: selling/distributing professional tools and consumables (power tools, PPE, fixings, abrasives…) — B2B
trade, sometimes a manufacturer. A **rational, repeated** purchase: the professional wants the right tool, in stock,
at the right price, delivered fast. A variable ticket per order, **strong repeat business**. The choice rests on range,
brands, availability, advice.

## Concept & angle
**The professionals' tool supplier**: a wide range, trusted brands, stock, technical advice, delivery. A
professional-efficient, reliable, no-frills tone. Sell availability, expertise and service (trade account, fast quote,
after-sales). No lifestyle emotion: here we talk range, brands, availability and price. The supplier's seriousness
comes first.

## STRUCTURE — the trade's information architecture (≠ equipement: here tools/consumables trade, not heavy installations)
1. **Hero**: a professional wall of tools/workshop + promise ("Your professional tool supplier") + CTA **"Request
   a quote / Trade account"** + phone.
2. **Ranges / categories**: families of tools and consumables (power tools, PPE, fixings, abrasives…).
3. **Brands carried**: a band of recognised brands — a pledge of quality and choice.
4. **Trade services**: trade account, fast quote, availability/stock, delivery, after-sales (the trade differentiator).
5. **Advice & expertise**: a team that knows the trade, demos, equipment loans (reassures the professional).
6. **Trust / references**: professional clients, branches/area, reviews — the supplier's seriousness.
7. **Final CTA**: "Open a trade account" / "Request a quote".

## Mood
Professional-efficient, robust. A dense, readable grotesque (Fakt, Suisse Intl, GT America, Geist) × mono for
references/specs. An industrial palette (black/orange or the brand's), frank contrast. Minimal motion, crisp reveals,
dense but clear.

## Avoid
A "lifestyle/editorial" tone. A messy, unreadable catalogue. Hiding the trade services (account, availability,
after-sales = the arguments). Confusing it with equipement (installed machines) or ferronnerie/metallerie
(manufacturers).

## Photos
Real tools and consumables, workshop/shop, PPE in use, brands. Sharp and readable.
The real stock/shop > generic tool stock images.
