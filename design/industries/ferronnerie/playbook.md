# ferronnerie — playbook (READ by the composer AND the filler)

> The **STRUCTURE and the concept come from HERE**. The trade decides the information architecture; fonts + archetype = flavour.

## What this trade sells
**Art ironwork**: bespoke wrought iron, decorative banisters and railings, gates, grilles, glass roofs, art
furniture. Ticket €2-40k, an **aesthetic and heritage** purchase. The client chooses on the **style**, the craft
gesture and the uniqueness of the piece. Closer to the artist-craftsman than to the locksmith.

## Concept & angle
**Iron worked as a noble material**: the hand, the forge, the ornament, the unique piece that lasts for generations.
An art-craftsman's tone, elegant and proud of the trade. Sell bespoke ornamental work and the craft (forging,
repoussé, patina), not hardware. The workshop's signature matters.

## STRUCTURE — the trade's information architecture (≠ metallerie: here art/ornament, not functional metal structure)
1. **Hero**: a magnificent piece of ironwork (a forged banister, an ornate gate) + promise ("Bespoke art ironwork")
   + CTA **"Imagine my piece / Quote"**.
2. **The craft**: forge, wrought iron, patina, repoussé — the craft gesture (the emotional heart).
3. **Creations**: banisters/railings, gates/grilles, glass roofs, furniture, heritage restoration (types).
4. **Projects**: a gallery of real pieces, forging and patina details — the proof of art, shown big.
5. **Made to measure**: from the drawing to the installation, a unique piece thought through with the client (the
   differentiator).
6. **Workshop & trust**: the forge, the craftsman, monument restoration/references, testimonials.
7. **Final CTA**: "Talk about my piece" / bespoke quote.

## Mood
Art craft, elegant and patinated. A serif with character (Reckless, Signifier, Times Now, Caslon Ionic) × a discreet
sans. An iron/matte black, patinated gold palette on a cream background. Slow motion, fades, close-ups of material.

## Avoid
A "locksmith call-out" or "industrial metalwork" tone (here it is art). Tiny galleries (the forged detail must be
seen). Purely technical vocabulary before the emotion of the piece.

## Photos
Real forged pieces in close-up (banisters, gates, glass roofs), forging/patina details, the workshop-forge.
The gesture and the material shown big > renders or stock photo.
