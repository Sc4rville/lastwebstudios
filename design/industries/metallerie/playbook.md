# metallerie — playbook (READ by the composer AND the filler)

> The **STRUCTURE and the concept come from HERE**. The trade decides the information architecture; fonts + archetype = flavour.

## What this trade sells
**Metalwork / locksmith-metalwork**: bespoke metal work — railings, metal staircases, workshop-style glass walls,
gates, structures, mezzanines, steel joinery. A mix of homeowners (decor/steel glass walls) and businesses/construction
(structures, railings). Ticket €2-50k. The choice rests on manufacturing quality, design and installation.

## Concept & angle
**Bespoke metal, designed and structural**: the glass wall people dream of, the steel staircase, the clean railing —
designed, made in the workshop, installed cleanly. A contemporary workshop tone, precise, a touch industrial-chic.
Sell the mastery (design → manufacture → installation) and the modern steel look. Different from art (ferronnerie):
here clean lines, functional and contemporary.

## STRUCTURE — the trade's information architecture (≠ ferronnerie: here structural/contemporary metal, not forged ornament)
1. **Hero**: a strong metal piece (glass wall, steel staircase, railing) + promise ("Bespoke metalwork in <area>")
   + CTA **"Quote / My project"**.
2. **Work**: railings, metal staircases, workshop-style glass walls, gates, structures/mezzanines (types).
3. **Made to measure & workshop**: design → steel manufacture (welding/powder coating) → installation (the
   differentiator).
4. **Projects**: a gallery of installed work (glass walls and staircases = very visual) — the proof, shown big.
5. **Homeowners & businesses**: decor/fit-out vs construction/structures (the visitor recognises themselves).
6. **Trust**: an equipped workshop, 10-year liability insurance, qualifications, testimonials.
7. **Final CTA**: "Study my metal project" / quote.

## Mood
A contemporary workshop, industrial-chic. A crisp, slightly cold grotesque (Monument Grotesk, GT America, Geist, Fakt)
× mono for labels. A black/grey steel, glass palette, 1 accent. Sober motion, crisp reveals, controlled lines.

## Avoid
The "art wrought iron / patina" tone (that is ferronnerie). Confusion with locksmith call-outs (see securite).
Tiny galleries (glass walls/staircases must be seen). Construction jargon before the look of the work.

## Photos
Real metal work: glass walls, steel staircases, railings, structures, installed in situ. Shown big.
Welding/coating details. Real projects > 3D renders.
