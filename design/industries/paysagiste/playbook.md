# paysagiste — playbook (READ by the composer AND the filler)

> The **STRUCTURE and the concept come from HERE**. The trade decides the information architecture; fonts + archetype = flavour.

## What this trade sells
**Creating and maintaining gardens/outdoor spaces**: landscaping, terraces, fences, planting, irrigation,
maintenance. Ticket €3-80k for creation; **maintenance = recurring** (contracts). An **aspirational** purchase (the
dream garden) for creation, a practical one for maintenance. The choice rests on the projects, taste and trust.

## Concept & angle
**The garden turned into a living space**: a designed, living outdoor space that changes with the seasons. A
nature-elegant, lively, generous tone. Sell the projection ("your dream garden") + control of the green job + the
maintenance that keeps it beautiful (the recurring part). The plants and the ground before/after do all the work.

## STRUCTURE — the trade's information architecture (≠ terrassement: here the garden/plants/landscaping, not earthworks alone)
1. **Aspirational hero**: a magnificent garden/terrace (light, plants) + promise ("Landscaper in <area>")
   + CTA **"My project / Free quote"**.
2. **Landscape creation**: design, terraces/paths, fences, planting, ponds/lighting (the dream).
3. **Projects**: a gallery of created gardens (before/after ground → garden = very strong) — the proof, shown big.
4. **A controlled project**: study/plan → preparation → landscaping → planting → finishing.
5. **Maintenance & contracts**: mowing, pruning, seasonal work, annual contracts (the recurring part — a business
   differentiator).
6. **Trust**: team, label/qualification, testimonials, service area.
7. **Final CTA**: "Study my garden" / free quote.

## Mood
Nature-elegant, lively and fresh. A soft serif or an organic grotesque (Garnett, Onest, Reckless, TWK Everett) × a
clean sans. A palette of greens/earth/golden light. Fluid motion, soft reveals, light parallax on the plants.

## Avoid
A "raw construction" tone (this is living things and aesthetics). Hiding maintenance (a recurring asset). Confusing it
with terrassement (heavy outdoor earthworks). Galleries without before/after (the argument that converts best here).

## Photos
Gardens and terraces created, shown big (plants, light, outdoor lifestyle), ground before/after.
The real local jobs > generic garden stock images.
