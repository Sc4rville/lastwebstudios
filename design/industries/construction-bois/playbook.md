# construction-bois — playbook (READ by the composer AND the filler)

> The **STRUCTURE and the concept come from HERE**. The trade decides the information architecture; fonts + archetype = flavour.

## What this trade sells
The **timber-frame or log house/building**: ecological, healthy, high-performance construction. A big ticket
(€150-400k), a client often won over by **values** (ecology, comfort, a fast build). The choice rests on the look of
wood, thermal performance and trust in a sector that is still "niche".

## Concept & angle
**Wood as a life choice**: a healthy, warm, low-energy house, a clean and fast build. A nature-premium, committed,
bright tone. Sell the timber way of life + the technical performance (RE2020 in France, thermal lag, insulation).
Set it apart from concrete block: looks, ecology, comfort, fast off-site assembly.

## STRUCTURE — the trade's information architecture (≠ masonry construction: here the timber sector, values + performance)
1. **Aspirational hero**: a beautiful timber house (light, cladding, nature) + promise ("Timber houses in <area>")
   + CTA **"My timber project / Quote"**.
2. **Why wood**: healthy, ecological, low-energy, a fast and clean build (lifting the preconceptions).
3. **Our building systems**: timber frame, log/post-and-beam, extension, raising (orients).
4. **Projects**: a gallery of delivered timber houses (bright interiors + exterior cladding) — the proof.
5. **Performance & build**: RE2020, insulation, airtightness, fast off-site assembly (the differentiator).
6. **Trust**: 10-year liability insurance, PEFC/FSC certified timber, labels, residents' testimonials.
7. **Final CTA**: "Study my timber project" / quote.

## Mood
Nature-premium, warm and bright. A soft serif or an organic grotesque (Reckless, Garnett, Onest, TWK Everett)
× a clean sans. A light wood/linen/muted green palette. Fluid motion, soft reveals, light parallax on light/cladding.

## Avoid
A "raw construction/concrete" tone (it is the opposite of the argument). Confusing it with charpente (the frame alone)
or masonry construction. Hollow greenwashing: lean on concrete facts (certification, RE2020, thermal lag).

## Photos
Real timber houses: exterior cladding, bright interiors, the timber frame, integration with nature.
Real local work > 3D renders or a generic chalet stock photo.
