# securite — playbook (security / alarm / locksmithing) (READ by the composer AND the filler)

> The **STRUCTURE and the concept come from HERE**. The trade decides the information architecture; fonts + archetype = flavour.

## What this trade sells
**Security of property and people**: alarm, CCTV, access control, remote monitoring, locksmithing/reinforced doors,
call-outs. Two engines: **emergency** (locksmith, break-in) and **equipment project** (alarm/video). Ticket
€0.2-20k + **recurring remote monitoring**. The choice rests on **reassurance**, responsiveness, seriousness.

## Concept & angle
**Peace of mind**: "protected, monitored, helped fast". A reliable, reassuring, professional tone (not excessively
alarming). Sell security as peace of mind + responsiveness (emergency) + follow-up (remote monitoring). Fight the
mistrust of locksmiths (a clear quote, approval, an identified local company).

## STRUCTURE — the trade's information architecture (≠ metallerie: here protection/alarm/call-outs, not metal work)
1. **Hero**: a protection/secured-home visual + promise ("Security & call-outs in <area>") + CTA
   **"Quote / Emergency"** + a **clearly visible phone** (emergencies call).
2. **Solutions**: alarm, CCTV, access control, remote monitoring, locksmithing/reinforced doors (by need).
3. **Emergency & call-outs**: fast intervention (door opening, after a break-in) — trigger #1.
4. **Remote monitoring / follow-up**: 24/7 monitoring, app, contracts (the recurring part — a differentiator).
5. **Projects / installations**: equipment installed (homes, shops) — the proof of seriousness.
6. **Trust**: approval/certification (APSAD, CNAPS in France), insurance, a clear quote, a local company, reviews.
7. **Final CTA**: "Free quote" / "Call us".

## Mood
Reliable, reassuring, crisp. A solid, clear grotesque (Suisse Intl, Geist, Fakt, GT America) × mono for labels.
A trust-blue/anthracite palette + 1 accent. Sober motion, crisp reveals — seriousness and readability before effect.

## Avoid
An anxiety-inducing/aggressive look. Hiding the phone (emergency). Vague promises without approvals (trust is
everything here). Confusing it with metallerie (locksmithing = call-outs/security here, not art work).

## Photos
Real installations (alarms, cameras, locks/reinforced doors fitted), a call-out, the monitoring centre.
Reassuring real photos > an anxiety-inducing burglar stock photo.
