# plombier — playbook (plumber)

> The **STRUCTURE and the concept come from HERE**. The trade decides the information architecture; fonts + archetype = flavour. Write the site in the agency's language (LANGUAGE).

## What this trade sells
**Emergencies fixed fast and cleanly** (leak, water heater, unblocking) and bathroom jobs. A small to medium ticket, a decision in a few minutes: the site must **capture the call**.

## Concept & angle
Reassure and get the call: "repairs today, price given before we start". A direct, local, human tone (the craftsman's face).

## STRUCTURE — the trade's information architecture
1. **Call hero**: promise (fast repairs, a clear quote) + area + a **giant call button** and opening hours.
2. **Call-outs**: leak, water heater, unblocking, bathroom, renovation (short cards, "from" prices if provided).
3. **How it works**: call → diagnosis → price given → work done (reassures on the price).
4. **Proof**: reviews, 10-year liability insurance, guarantees, photos of real jobs.
5. **Service area**: towns covered (local SEO).
6. **Final CTA**: call + a short form.

## Mood
Clean, confident, readable on mobile above all (the call comes from a phone). Frank blues or warm neutrals, a crisp display.

## Avoid
A "corporate" brochure site. Hiding the number. Stock photos of taps.

## Photos
The prospect's real jobs shown big; no stock images. With no usable photo: illustrate the trade (SVG, textures), invent nothing.
